# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

TrendLoom stores extension settings and download history in the browser's local extension storage. The extension does not send usage analytics or error reports to TrendLoom or an analytics provider.

When you use the downloader, your browser requests media from Instagram and its content delivery domains. Those requests go directly to the domains listed in the extension manifest and are subject to Instagram's privacy policy. TrendLoom does not proxy those requests.

## Data stored locally

- Extension settings and preferences
- Download history and queue status

This information remains in the browser profile and is removed when you clear it or uninstall the extension. The extension does not collect Instagram passwords or send your download history to TrendLoom.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** saves preferences and download history locally.
- **Instagram and media host access:** lets the extension read media on Instagram pages and access Instagram's media delivery domains.

The extension's background service worker does not report install events, browsing activity, downloads, or errors to third parties.
